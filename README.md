# SGX3 Coding Institute & Hackathon 2026

## Build. Learn. Innovate.

The **SGX3 Coding Institute** is an immersive virtual training program designed for **undergraduate and graduate students** interested in **science gateway development, HPC, and AI-driven workflows**.

Through **live coding sessions**, hands-on exercises, and collaborative learning, participants will gain the practical skills needed to design, build, and maintain modern scientific applications.

## Program Dates

**June 1–26, 2026**

- **Training:** June 1–19, 2026  
- **Hackathon:** June 22–26, 2026

## Participant Benefits

- **$2,000 stipend**
- Real-world **HPC and AI experience**
- Hands-on **software development training**
- Collaborative **team-based hackathon**
- Professional development workshops

## What You’ll Learn

- Intro to **Scientific Computing & Computational Thinking**
- **HPC using Python**
- **SIR Model** pseudocode development
- **Software Engineering Design**
- Containers with **Flask and Redis**
- Practical workflows for building and maintaining science gateways

## Training Experience

The institute combines:

- Short, focused tutorials
- Live coding instruction
- Hands-on practical exercises

You won’t just learn — **you’ll build**.

## Hackathon Experience

During the final week, participants will put their new skills into practice in an engaging team-based hackathon.

- Work with fellow participants
- Apply newly learned coding skills
- Complete a challenge with your team
- Gain experience in collaboration, problem-solving, and project development
