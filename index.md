# Build. Learn. Innovate.

The **SGX3 Coding Institute** is an immersive virtual training program designed for **undergraduate and graduate students** interested in **science gateway development, HPC, and AI-driven workflows**.

Through live coding sessions, hands-on exercises, and collaborative learning, participants gain the practical skills needed to design, build, and maintain modern scientific applications.

## Hackathon Experience

<span class="section-tag hackathon-tag">Final Week · June 22–26</span>

During the final week, participants put their new skills into practice in an engaging team-based hackathon:

- Work alongside fellow participants on a shared challenge
- Apply the coding skills developed across the training weeks
- Experience real collaboration, problem-solving, and sprint-style delivery
- Gain a tangible project for your portfolio
