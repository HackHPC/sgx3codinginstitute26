# Empowering the Next Generation of Computational Scientists

The **SGX3 Coding Institute 2026** is a premier workforce development initiative led by the **Science Gateways Community Institute (SGX3)**, a National Science Foundation (NSF) Center of Excellence. This intensive four-week program equips a diverse cohort of undergraduate students with the critical skills required to navigate modern Science Gateways, High-Performance Computing (HPC), and Artificial Intelligence (AI) ecosystems — bridging the gap between theoretical computer science and applied research.

  <div class="curriculum-item">
    <h3>Git &amp; Version Control</h3>

  </div>
  <div class="curriculum-item">
    <h3>APIs &amp; Data Pipelines</h3>
    <p>Query live web <strong>APIs</strong> &mdash; including the Wikipedia API &mdash; and parse complex <strong>JSON data structures</strong> to feed computational models</p>
  </div>
  <div class="curriculum-item">
    <h3>Local AI &amp; LLMs</h3>
    <p>Deploy and manage <strong>local AI inference servers</strong> using Ollama and LM Studio &mdash; understanding AI mechanics rather than relying on commercial black-box tools</p>
  </div>
  <div class="curriculum-item">
    <h3>NLP &amp; Vector Embeddings</h3>
    <p>Use <strong><code>spaCy</code></strong> and <strong><code>sentence-transformers</code></strong> to convert raw text into high-dimensional embeddings and measure semantic similarity mathematically</p>

  <div class="curriculum-item">

    <p>Program physics-based <strong>Knowledge Graphs</strong> using <code>NetworkX</code> &mdash; transforming unstructured scientific text into structured, visual data networks</p>
  </div>
  <div class="curriculum-item">
    <h3>RAG &amp; GraphRAG</h3>
    <p>Integrate Knowledge Graphs with <strong>Retrieval-Augmented Generation</strong> architectures to build AI agents that extract deterministic facts while mitigating hallucinations</p>

## Mastering HPC &amp; Science Gateways

<div class="highlight-box teal">
  <p>Students gain direct, hands-on experience with world-class HPC infrastructure at the <strong>Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC)</strong>. The curriculum demystifies supercomputing architecture, covers secure SSH access, Linux command-line proficiency, and collaborative development with Git &mdash; preparing participants for real enterprise and research environments.</p>
</div>

## Pioneering AI, NLP &amp; Knowledge Graphs

<div class="highlight-box" style="border-left-color: var(--purple);">
  <p>A core priority of the 2026 program is <strong>&ldquo;AI Readiness&rdquo;</strong> &mdash; ensuring students understand the mathematical mechanics of AI. Participants use <code>spaCy</code> and <code>sentence-transformers</code> for tokenization and vectorization, build <code>NetworkX</code> Knowledge Graphs, and wire them into <strong>RAG and GraphRAG</strong> pipelines to create intelligent, hallucination-resistant AI agents.</p>

## Hackathon Experience

<span class="section-tag hackathon-tag">ADMI Hackathon &middot; June 22&ndash;26</span>

The institute culminates in the **ADMI Hackathon**, where collaborative teams synthesize their new NLP, API, and HPC proficiencies to engineer **autonomous Knowledge Graph generators**. By transforming unstructured scientific text into structured, visual data networks, participants prove their readiness to build and sustain the next generation of Science Gateways and AI-driven research tools.

- Synthesize NLP, API querying, and HPC skills under a real deadline
- Build autonomous Knowledge Graph generators as a team
- Transform unstructured scientific text into structured data networks
- Demonstrate readiness for careers in national scientific infrastructure
